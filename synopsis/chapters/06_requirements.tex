\chapter{Hardware \& Software Requirements}
\label{chap:requirements}

The development, deployment, and simulation of the Reforge platform require a robust engineering stack spanning blockchain execution clients, high-throughput backend services, interactive user interfaces, and scientific research environments.

\section{Software Requirements}
The software ecosystem is decomposed into four functional tiers, as detailed in Table~\ref{tab:software_requirements}.

\begin{table}[htbp]
\centering
\small

\textbf{Subsystem} & \textbf{Technology} & \textbf{Version} & \textbf{Role \& Functionality} \\

\section{Hardware Requirements}
Executing local EVM test forks, running asynchronous WebSocket listeners, and processing large-scale Monte Carlo price trajectory simulations necessitate high-performance workstation hardware. Table~\ref{tab:hardware_requirements} outlines the minimum and recommended specifications.

\begin{table}[htbp]
\centering
\small
\caption{Hardware specifications for development and simulation nodes.}
\label{tab:hardware_requirements}
\begin{tabular}{p{3.2cm}|p{5.2cm}|p{5.2cm}}
\hline
\textbf{Component} & \textbf{Minimum Specification} & \textbf{Recommended Specification} \\
\hline
\textbf{Processor (CPU)} & Quad-core Intel Core i5 or Ryzen 5 & 8-core Intel Core i7/i9 or Ryzen 7/9 \\
\textbf{System Memory} & 16 GB DDR4 & 32 GB DDR5 (for in-memory simulation) \\
\textbf{Storage (SSD)} & 256 GB NVMe SSD & 1 TB NVMe PCIe 4.0 SSD \\
\textbf{Network} & 50 Mbps broadband & 100+ Mbps low-latency fiber \\
\textbf{Operating System} & Windows 11 / Ubuntu 22.04 LTS & Ubuntu 24.04 LTS or WSL2 \\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{table}
